Round-5: The Defect Was the Emission Policy, Not the Features

Reproduction first. scripts/cache_fold_models.py re-trained raw19 / multi25 / multi30 / random02 on the frozen masked protocol and matched evidence/holdout_masked.json on every fold of every arm to full float64 precision (multi25 = 0.09071277311870418). Rounds 1–4 are verified reproducible. That verification exposed the real defect: on the same protocol the uniform random 2 % control scores 0.13788 — better than every learned arm we have built.

The official metric gives a truth pixel credit if any predicted pixel falls within 300 m (3 px) of it, and takes max over that 5 × 5 cell. Two predictions inside one cell therefore add false-positive mass and add zero true-positive credit. Every round 1–4 arm — and the archived 06ca61e5 artifact — emitted top-k by probability, which piles mass exactly where it is wasted. The sparsest emission that can still credit every truth pixel is one with no two predictions in a 5 × 5 block: non-maximum suppression at radius 2, density 1/25.

Why H28 should catch faults missing from USGS/INGENIOUS: Hydrothermal alteration destroys magnetite along fault damage zones, creating disrupted magnetic texture (high local variance) rather than clean edge. USGS QFFD requires surface geomorphic scarps; subsurface alteration halo beneath non-magnetic alluvium is invisible to surface mapping but visible to magnetics. H28 uses mag_anom (B1) local standard deviation in 11 px window, distinct from all prior transforms (max |corr| 0.384 vs tmi_hg). Layers: B1 only. Physical signature: texture disruption, not edge.

Other R6 candidates: H25 seismic proximity (B10 deq + B7/B8/B4) — first use of deq_n100a15, targets blind seismically active faults; H26 gravity-slope curvature (B5 + B12) — buried density step; H27 conductivity anisotropy (B17) — fluid-filled conduit fabric; H29 grav-mag decorrelation (B13×B2) — correlation breakdown in damage zones. All viable, none pass both protocols except H28. External H30 1m DEM (USGS 3DEP via AWS S3 s3://usgs-3dep public domain) and H31 Sentinel-2 L2A (s3://sentinel-cogs free/open) verified free/open but transport blocked in-sandbox.

Round-5 geological candidates: honest negative. H20 magnetic-basement step, H21 two-depth-surface step coincidence, H22 local singularity exponent and H23 asymmetric-step detector were preregistered (register) after identity checks showed that tc (B5) is the official top-of-crustal magnetic source depth estimate, not the "tilt angle or total curvature" its raster description claims (strictly positive, median 18.48, correlation −0.0012 with |tilt|). With the emission policy fixed, no channel cleared the ≥3/4-fold gate on both protocols (best: H22 3/4 dense, 2/4 sparse; H15 2/4 sparse). All arms now land within ±0.0008 (dense) and ±0.0018 (sparse) of each other — an order of magnitude below the emission effect. That is the actionable conclusion: stop paying for small feature gains while the decision layer throws away a factor of 2.5.
